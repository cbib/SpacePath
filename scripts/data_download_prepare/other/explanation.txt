
   * Brain cancer samples from Ravi, Heiland, et al., Cell, 2022 *

The raw imzML + ibd files were kindly provided by the authors after request.
The adata objects (.h5ad files) were generated as follows:

Part A:

The rscript.zip contains the R script to perform spectra warping (with MALDIquant), and also:
    a.corrected batch effect.
    b.repeated the rows in the quantifications data to accomodate to the fact that several HMDB identifiers correspond to one m/z.
    c.assigned HMDB identifiers (instead m/z names (*)) to the data.

Finally it saves the .tsv files of both intensities, and 'x,y' coordinates, in separate files, for each patient sample.

Part B: 

The script `customGB-tsvs-reformat.py` does the following:

1) Reassigns m/z names to the quantifications tables, using the annotations file (.csv).
2) Deduplicates the rows.
2) Saves the resulting adata objects (.h5ad files)

   
- - 

Notes: 
(*) Such m/z names are written in numbers but are considered as discrete variables in downstream analysis.
